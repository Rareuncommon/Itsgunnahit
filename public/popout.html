How to use it while playing

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on your gaming PC and click Pop out over game.
  2. Drag the little window to a corner (top-left works well, away from the minimap) and resize it.
  3. In Fortnite, go to Settings → Display → Window Mode and pick Windowed Fullscreen. In regular Fullscreen mode the game hides every other window.
  4. Press your Stream Deck button. The counter updates within a second and pops on every new chest.

Streaming? Use the OBS overlay as a Browser Source instead, so viewers see it too. On console there's no way to draw over the game, so keep this page open on a phone or second screen.
